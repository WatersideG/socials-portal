# Waterside Group — Competitor Landscape Research

Compiled: July 12, 2026. Method: live web research (search + source pages) identifying real, named local/regional competitors for each Waterside Group brand, plus a review of tools and best practices for tracking competitor social/search performance in a dashboard. All competitors listed are actual operating businesses in the same geography and category. Websites are given where verified in sources; a few URLs are marked (unverified) where the business is confirmed but the exact domain was not independently confirmed.

## COMPETITOR TRACKING: TOOLS & WHAT'S OBTAINABLE WITHOUT THEIR ACCOUNT ACCESS

### Free / platform-native tools

**Meta Ad Library** ([transparency.meta.com — Ad Library tools](https://transparency.meta.com/researchtools/ad-library-tools); access at facebook.com/ads/library)
- Completely free and public; no account or API key needed. Shows every *active* ad a Page is running across Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, Audience Network — creative, copy, start date, platforms, and formats ([Simplified guide](https://simplified.com/blog/ai-social-media/meta-ad-library), [Commit Agency](https://commitagency.com/insights/using-the-meta-ad-library-for-competitor-research-and-creative-inspiration/)).
- Obtainable: whether a competitor is advertising at all, how many ads, how long each has run (long-running = likely working), seasonal messaging, offers, landing pages.
- Not obtainable: clicks, conversions, spend (except political), exact targeting; non-political ads disappear from the library once inactive ([AdLibrary.com guide](https://adlibrary.com/guides/how-to-use-meta-ad-library-competitor-research)).
- Dashboard use: monthly screenshot/log of active ad count + themes per competitor.

**Instagram native Competitive Insights** — launched Nov 3, 2025: compare up to 10 accounts in-app on follower growth, posting frequency, Reels output, and boosted posts over 30–90 days, free ([StoryChief 2026 guide](https://storychief.io/blog/instagram-competitor-insights)).

**Google Ads Auction Insights** ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2579754?hl=en))
- Requires only *your own* Google Ads account (no competitor access). For Search: impression share, overlap rate, outranking share, position-above rate, top-of-page rate, absolute top-of-page rate; for Shopping: impression share, overlap rate, outranking share ([Store Growers](https://www.storegrowers.com/google-ads-auction-insights/), [Karooya 2026 guide](https://www.karooya.com/blog/google-ads-auction-insights-2026-guide-to-tracking-and-outmaneuvering-your-competitors/)).
- Reveals who is actually bidding against each brand (e.g., which Falmouth restaurants bid on "waterfront dining Falmouth") and whether their pressure is rising — segment by time to trend it in a dashboard.

**Google Trends / Share of Search** — free, weekly-frequency, back to 2004. Put brand + competitor names in one Trends view; Share of Search % = your brand index ÷ total index of tracked brands. Correlates with and often *leads* market share ([Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/blog/share-of-search/), [Kantar](https://www.kantar.com/north-america/Inspiration/Analytics/Demystifying-share-of-search), [Shopify](https://www.shopify.com/blog/share-of-search)). Treat as directional; track the multi-month trend, not single months.

**Google Maps / Business Profile review benchmarking**
- Public for every competitor: star rating, total review count, review recency, and (by logging counts over time) review velocity — reviews gained per 30 days. Velocity and recency often outweigh total count for map-pack rankings ([Local Falcon](https://www.localfalcon.com/blog/what-is-google-review-velocity-and-why-does-it-matter-for-businesses), [Local Dominator](https://localdominator.co/review-velocity-local-seo/), [Search Engine Land GBP audit](https://searchengineland.com/google-business-profile-audit-local-rankings-472990)).
- Tools that automate competitor review/rank tracking: Local Falcon, Places Scout, Whitespark, Semrush Local ([Semrush local SEO tools](https://www.semrush.com/blog/local-seo-tools/)).

### Paid social benchmarking (public-data based)

All of these work purely from public profile data — no competitor login needed:

- **Metricool** — competitor tracking built into scheduling/analytics suite; free plan tracks 5 competitor profiles, Starter (~$22/mo) up to 100. Good budget fit for a multi-brand portfolio ([metricool.com](https://metricool.com/competitor-analysis-tools/), [CompetitorTools review](https://competitortools.io/metricool)).
- **Socialinsider** — deep cross-channel competitor benchmarking, content-pillar analysis (which topics/formats win), from ~$99/mo ([socialinsider.io](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-competitor-analysis), [vs Rival IQ comparison](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/socialinsider-vs-rival-iq/)).
- **Rival IQ** — competitive "landscapes" with automated alerts on competitors' high-performing posts and industry benchmarks; from ~$239/mo; no LinkedIn competitor data. Free head-to-head reports and the annual industry benchmark report are useful even without a subscription ([rivaliq.com free analytics](https://www.rivaliq.com/free-social-media-analytics/)).
- Roundups comparing the field: [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/best-social-media-analytics-tools/), [Sprout Social IG competitor reports](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/instagram-competitors-report/), [NapoleonCat](https://napoleoncat.com/blog/analyze-competitors-on-instagram/).

### Search/web intelligence

- **Semrush / Ahrefs** — competitor domain comparison: estimated organic traffic, top pages, ranking keywords, Keyword Gap / Content Gap (keywords they rank for that you don't), backlink profiles; Semrush adds paid-search/advertising research ([Style Factory comparison](https://www.stylefactoryproductions.com/blog/ahrefs-vs-semrush), [OneLittleWeb](https://onelittleweb.com/top-tools/semrush-vs-ahrefs/), [Embryo](https://embryo.com/blog/semrush-vs-ahrefs/)). Note: all traffic figures are estimates; for small local sites treat as relative, not absolute.
- **Similarweb** — traffic and engagement estimates for competitor domains ([similarweb.com competitive analysis](https://www.similarweb.com/corp/web/competitive-analysis/)). Accuracy caveat: strongest for sites with ~5K–100K monthly users (SparkToro found it the most accurate of the estimators); it materially overstates small/low-traffic local sites — often 2–3x — and may not report tiny sites at all. Use for relative benchmarking of the larger competitors (resorts, chains, outlet malls), not small restaurants ([PerfectYourSEO](https://perfectyourseo.com/how-accurate-is-similarweb/), [Ivanhoe research](https://ivanhoe.pro/how-accurate-is-similarweb-data/)).

| Followers & follower growth | Public profiles; Metricool/Socialinsider/Rival IQ track over time | High (exact) |
| Posting frequency & content mix (photo/carousel/Reel) | Public profiles / benchmarking tools / IG Competitive Insights | High (exact) |
| Engagement on public posts (likes, comments) & engagement rate per follower | Public posts; tools compute rates | High for likes/comments; shares/saves/reach are estimates or unavailable |
| Ad presence + creative + ad longevity | Meta Ad Library (free); Google Ads Transparency Center | High for active ads; no performance data |
| Paid-search pressure (impression share, overlap, outranking) | Google Ads Auction Insights (your account) | High, but only vs. co-bidders |
| Estimated website traffic & top pages/keywords | Semrush/Ahrefs/Similarweb | Directional only; weak for very small local sites |
| Share of search (brand demand vs. competitors) | Google Trends (free) | Directional; leading indicator of market share |
| Review count, rating, review velocity, recency | Google Maps public data; Local Falcon/Whitespark/Semrush Local | High (exact counts; velocity = your logged deltas) |
| What you CANNOT get | — | Their impressions/reach internals, conversions, spend, email/CRM, story metrics, exact demographics |

### Practical dashboard recipe for Waterside
1. Per brand, track 3–5 named competitors above.
2. Monthly (automatable via Metricool on a budget): followers, follower growth %, posts/week, avg engagement/post, top post.
3. Monthly manual/logged: Google review count + rating (compute velocity), Meta Ad Library active-ad count + theme notes.
4. Quarterly: Google Trends share-of-search per category cluster; Auction Insights per active Google Ads campaign; Semrush domain compare for the bigger competitors (Settlers Green, Loon/RiverWalk, Southport, Royal Shell, Ryan Family).
